# Accurate metadata

**Apple App Review Guideline 2.3** · Google Play: Metadata policy. Read the official text: [App Review Guidelines](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/#accurate-metadata), [Google Play policy center](https://play.google.com/about/developer-content-policy/).

## What it means

The name, description, screenshots and previews must show the app as it is, without features it does not have or misleading claims.

## Why Despia apps hit it

Store screenshots made from the website or a design file instead of the shipped app; descriptions that promise features behind a future update.

## How to fix it

The fix is in the app's content and store listing, not in an API.


## Reply to the reviewer

Adapt it to what you actually changed; reply in App Store Connect (Resolution Center) only after the fix is in the build you submit.

```text
Thank you. The screenshots now come from the current build on [devices], and the description lists only features available in this version.
```
