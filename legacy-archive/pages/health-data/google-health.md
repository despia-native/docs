# Google Health

> Google Health Connect integration for Android is coming soon to Despia.

Google Health Connect support for Android is on the roadmap. Read and write health and fitness data on Android using the same patterns as Apple Health - steps, heart rate, sleep, workouts, and more.

To be notified when it ships, reach out at [support@despia.com](mailto:support@despia.com).
